# Connecto — Incubator Demo Script

## Objective

Demonstrate the core problem and solution in 3–5 minutes.

Do not attempt to show every feature.

## Opening — 20 Seconds

"Connecto is a B2B ordering platform for small retailers and their suppliers. The goal is to reduce the dependence on manual order collection through calls, messages, and periodic sales visits."

## Demo Flow

### 1. Shop Owner Login
Show role selection and login.

### 2. Dashboard
Show:
- Orders
- Pending orders
- Basic business summary

Do not spend time on analytics here.

### 3. Supplier Discovery
Open Suppliers.

Say:
"A retailer can see suppliers available to them and browse their catalogs digitally."

### 4. Product Selection
Open one supplier.

Add several products.

Then open another supplier and add products.

Say:
"The retailer can build one shopping session across multiple suppliers."

### 5. Cart
Show products grouped supplier-wise.

Say:
"Although the retailer sees one cart, the system understands that fulfillment happens supplier by supplier."

### 6. Order Creation
Place the order/request.

Avoid claiming real payment settlement.

### 7. Orders
Show supplier-wise orders.

### 8. Supplier Login
Switch to supplier.

Show incoming order.

Update:
Pending → Accepted → Preparing → Out for Delivery → Delivered

### 9. Close

"The first version focuses on digitizing the retailer-to-supplier ordering workflow. Payments, inventory integrations, delivery optimization, AI insights, and broader manufacturer participation can be added after validating the core workflow."

## If Asked: "Why will distributors use it?"

Answer honestly:

"The hypothesis is that distributors can reduce manual order collection and get a structured view of incoming retailer demand. We are validating that assumption with distributors before building deeper distributor-side automation."

Do not claim proven traction unless it actually exists.

## If Asked: "Why not WhatsApp?"

"WhatsApp is useful for communication, but it is not designed as a structured procurement workflow. Connecto's hypothesis is that product catalogs, quantities, supplier-wise orders, status tracking, and analytics can be handled in one structured workflow."

This is a hypothesis until validated.

## If Asked: "Why not IndiaMART/Udaan/ONDC?"

Do not claim competitors do not solve the problem.

Instead:

"Connecto is initially exploring a narrower workflow: recurring FMCG replenishment for small retailers, with a very simple, local-language-friendly experience and supplier-side order management. We still need to validate whether that focus creates enough differentiation."

## If Asked: "Is this already an MVP?"

Say:

"This is currently a prototype. The MVP will come after testing the workflow with real retailers and suppliers."
