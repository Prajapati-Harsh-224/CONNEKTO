# Connecto — Product Roadmap

## Stage 0 — Prototype

Goal:
Demonstrate the core workflow.

Build:
- Role selection
- Authentication UI
- Shop owner
- Supplier discovery
- Product catalog
- Multi-supplier cart
- Order creation
- Supplier incoming orders
- Order status
- Basic analytics

Do not block the prototype on:
- Real payments
- Brand contracts
- Live inventory
- AI
- Delivery optimization

## Stage 1 — Validation

Target:
- Speak with shop owners.
- Speak with distributors/wholesalers.
- Observe their current ordering process.
- Test the prototype.

Questions:
- How are orders currently collected?
- How frequently does a sales representative visit?
- What happens when a retailer needs an urgent reorder?
- What information does the distributor need?
- Would digital orders reduce work?
- What would prevent them from using Connecto?
- Would they pay for it?
- Who actually makes the purchasing decision?

Success is not "people like the idea."

Success is evidence of:
- Repeated pain
- Willingness to change behavior
- Willingness to test
- Ideally, willingness to pay

## Stage 2 — MVP

Only build features validated by users.

Potential:
- Real accounts
- Real supplier catalogs
- Real orders
- Notifications
- Basic order management
- Basic analytics
- Local language

## Stage 3 — Business Model Validation

Potential models:
- Supplier SaaS subscription
- Retailer subscription
- Transaction fee
- Supplier lead/order fee
- Premium analytics

Do not choose a model before understanding who receives measurable value.

## Stage 4 — Payments

Potential:
- Online payment
- Supplier-wise settlement
- Refunds
- Reconciliation
- Invoices

This requires careful legal, tax, and payment-provider design.

## Stage 5 — Scale

Potential:
- Inventory integration
- Delivery routing
- Brand dashboards
- Demand analytics
- Promotions
- AI assistant
- Gruh Udyog expansion
- Multi-city operations
