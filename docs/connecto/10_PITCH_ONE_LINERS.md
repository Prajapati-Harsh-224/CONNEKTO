# Connecto — Positioning & Pitch

## One Sentence

Connecto helps small retailers digitally discover products, place orders with FMCG suppliers, and track those orders in one simple workflow.

## 15-Second Version

"Small retailers often depend on sales reps, calls, and WhatsApp to reorder FMCG products. Connecto gives them a simple digital way to browse supplier catalogs, place orders, and track fulfillment, while giving suppliers structured incoming orders."

## 30-Second Version

"Connecto is a B2B ordering platform focused on small retailers and FMCG suppliers. Instead of waiting for a sales representative or managing orders across calls and messages, a retailer can browse supplier products, add products from multiple suppliers, place a structured order, and track it. Suppliers receive those orders digitally and can manage their status. We are starting with the ordering workflow and will validate the model with real retailers and distributors before expanding into payments, inventory, AI, and delivery."

## Problem Statement

"Small retailers often have a fragmented and manual FMCG replenishment process, while distributors spend time collecting and organizing retailer orders."

## Solution

"Connecto digitizes the recurring retailer-to-supplier ordering workflow."

## What Connecto Is NOT Yet

- Not an official FMCG brand marketplace.
- Not a payment settlement network.
- Not a logistics company.
- Not a guaranteed inventory platform.
- Not yet a validated marketplace at scale.

## Founder Positioning

Do not say:
"I have built the future of FMCG."

Say:
"I identified a workflow problem, built a prototype to test the hypothesis, and am looking for mentorship and early users to validate and refine the model."

That is more credible at the current stage.
